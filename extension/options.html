Instant fix settings

When you press Alt+Shift+F or choose “I’m stuck — fix this page”, Curb Cut sends TypeSafe Jev a visible-tab screenshot and a short description of nearby page elements, including labels, layout, and the page path. Use instant fixes on public pages. A page with a populated editable field or sign-in form is skipped.

Repairs apply to the current tab. Press Escape to undo. Curb Cut queues the temporary rules and a generated task with your local Curb Cut server for Kane verification.
