Curb Cut settings

Instant fix

Off by default. When enabled, press Alt+Shift+F or choose “Try experimental instant fix” in the popup. Curb Cut checks the page and looks for repair candidates before capturing a JPEG of the visible tab.

TypeSafe receives the screenshot, page origin and path, title, viewport size and zoom, and details about the focused element, nearby controls and up to 16 repair candidates. Those details include accessible names or visible text, element types, positions and candidate repairs.

Use this on public HTTPS pages. Curb Cut skips sensitive URLs, sign-in and payment forms, and visible text fields containing values. A proposed fix applies temporarily in the current tab. Press Escape or choose Undo to remove it.

After applying a fix, Curb Cut sends the rule, its task description, the model and confidence to the local verification queue. If the server is unavailable, the extension saves that entry locally and retries. The screenshot stays out of the queue.

Live names

On public HTTPS pages, TypeSafe receives crops of unnamed controls and images, nearby text and clues about their purpose. It may also receive a visible text sample to identify the language. Names are temporary and unverified. Turn this off or pause the page to remove them.

TypeSafe connection

With a personal key, the extension sends requests directly to TypeSafe. With an empty key, it sends the capture and candidate selectors to the local Curb Cut server at 127.0.0.1:4317. Both routes keep the candidate selectors out of the TypeSafe request.
