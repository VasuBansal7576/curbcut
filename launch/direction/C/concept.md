# The corrections desk

A visitor writes to Mass.edu with a small, specific request. Their letter becomes a correction in the margin of the offending page. A patch can be published today while the original is being amended. The story ends when that margin can finally be left blank. This gives the source owner and the temporary nature of each fix a part in the composition.

## Palette and type

Newsprint #F6EEE2, brown ink #322822, correction red #A33423. Times New Roman headlines, Georgia body and italic marginalia. Preserve the color of the source pixels.

## Movement

A leader rule appears only when it connects a finding to its target. Insert the correction at the captured application time. Retract the note only after a clean unpatched replay.

## Sound

Read the proposed letter in a natural first-person voice. The reader speaks the changed label. Save the quietest moment for the clean page. No simulated typewriter ambience.

All first-person lines are proposed performance copy. The frames use original Mass.edu browser plates. Agent handoffs are editorial reconstructions from the failure record and bundled rule.
