Fix accessibility barriers in the browser, with evidence for every repair.

Curb Cut is a Chrome extension that applies accessibility repairs to public websites using reversible JSON rules. It targets the six failure types behind 96% of errors detected in WebAIM’s 2026 study. We preregistered 400 unseen Tranco sites and measured 76 local repairs across 118 tasks. Kane verified 7 of 15 sampled naming tasks in a Reading view replay with a sighted pointer control. Contrast and document language use local measurements. The extension bundles 105 active fixes. Its popup exports a Markdown report that gives the site owner the barrier, WCAG criterion, exact rule, and available recordings. Turn repairs off and on to repeat the check yourself. The dashboard links to the public task registration, measurements, and archived Kane recordings.
