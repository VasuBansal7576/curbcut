Fix accessibility barriers in the browser, with evidence for every repair.

Curb Cut applies reversible JSON fixes to public websites. It targets the six failure types behind 96% of WebAIM’s detected errors in 2026. The v6 study repaired 76 of 118 registered tasks locally across 400 scouted sites. V5 repaired 99 of 142 across 2,050 sites, including 51 of 89 multi-step journeys. Kane verified 11 of 20 randomly sampled v5 repairs and 7 of 15 v6 naming repairs with before, pointer control and after recordings.

The extension bundles 105 audited fixes, 61 Kane-verified and 44 with local proof. The local bundle includes 41 contrast fixes and three language fixes from development pages. The held-out language check repaired 0 of 20. The opt-in TypeSafe Jev experiment repaired 3 of 142 held-out barriers, all hover menus.
