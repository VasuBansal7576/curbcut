# Curb Cut landing page motion study

The five references came from Minimal Gallery, reached through Designeer. The Landing Love and Lapa Ninja directories returned a Cloudflare challenge during the isolated session. Each live reference capture used a fresh headless Chromium context at 1100 × 720. The opening, two wheel-scroll positions, and the recording for each site are in the ignored `.curbcut/site-benchmark/` directory. Files use the live site's hostname. ## Recording reviews

1. **First desktop and mobile cut.** The journal image and physical key gave the opening a recognizable silhouette at thumbnail size. Beside the reference clips, the generic rectangular curb looked inert. The Mass.edu hero photo competed with the tiny About item. The statistical field was almost invisible. Tightened the proof crops around About, changed the SVG mask so filled dots remain cobalt, and added a caster that rolls over the lowered curb. Moved the Jev label's travel inside the mobile viewport.
2. **Second desktop and mobile cut.** The larger menu crop made the open and closed states distinguishable. The phone still crossed multiple Kane steps while the visitor was reading their introduction. Gave mobile four successive frames with individual captions. On desktop the control buttons can hold any stage for inspection. Staggered the three replay posters vertically and timed the reader names and contrast sample to settle when centered. Replaced the footer's earlier film thumbnail with the new cobalt launch cover through the existing build.
3. **Final desktop and phone playback.** All four names now settle while the reader section is centered, and the contrast sample ends at 4.5 to 1. Widening the outlined specimen preserved the question mark. The phone recording shows each protocol frame in sequence. The replay preview originally opened on a white loading frame from the full recording. The six local previews now open directly on seven-second excerpts of the recorded action. Mass.edu starts at 92 and 73 seconds, Munich Airport at 45 and 46 seconds, and WoRMS at 70 and 60 seconds for before and after respectively. Activating a preview opens its complete source recording. The final ledger interaction opens Mass.edu and restores the full inventory.

The accent pairs used for text measure 6.46 to 1 for white on cobalt, 5.81 to 1 for lime on cobalt, 16.36 to 1 for ink on lime, and 5.92 to 1 for ink on coral. The contrast specimen has a high-contrast text transcription throughout. Its final gray measures 4.54 to 1 on white.

The opening journal cover is an original generated image. The built-in image generator was prompted for a landscape architecture-magazine photograph of a monumental coral concrete spiral staircase, pale lavender sky, deep shadows, and film grain, without lettering. The simulated publication's navigation and typography are live HTML. The Kane panels use crops from the linked evidence archives.
