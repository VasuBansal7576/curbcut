# Curb Cut launch posts

## Hacker News

**Show HN: Curb Cut fixes accessibility barriers in your browser**

Press Tab on Mass.edu. The About menu gets focus, but its links stay out of reach. Turn on Curb Cut and the same key opens the menu. On Munich Airport a screen reader can now identify the previous-slide button. [Watch the launch film](https://github.com/VasuBansal7576/curbcut/blob/main/launch/curbcut-launch.mp4)

The Curb Cut Chrome extension bundles 128 fixes. On November 24, 2026, the installed-extension audit recorded 98 primary passes and seven unconfirmed results across that day’s 105 fixes. [Live audit](https://github.com/VasuBansal7576/curbcut/blob/main/proof/reliability/submission-extension-e2e.json) The popup lets you inspect the evidence for a fix and send the proposed source change to the people who run the site. The film follows one Mass.edu keyboard task through Kane’s failed and successful attempts. [Evidence](https://github.com/VasuBansal7576/curbcut/blob/main/proof/README.md) · [Get Curb Cut for Chrome](https://github.com/VasuBansal7576/curbcut)

## X

Press Tab. The menu won’t open. Same site, same key, Curb Cut on. Now it opens. [15-second film](https://github.com/VasuBansal7576/curbcut/blob/main/launch/curbcut-launch-15s.mp4)

Curb Cut gives unnamed buttons a label, makes faint text readable and puts the evidence for each shipped fix in the extension. On unfamiliar sites, the optional Jev experiment can name a control or leave it alone when uncertain. [Get Curb Cut for Chrome](https://github.com/VasuBansal7576/curbcut)

## LinkedIn

On Munich Airport, the previous-slide control had no accessible name. With Curb Cut its name is “Vorherige Folie,” German for “previous slide.” On Meridiano, the opt-in Jev experiment found names for the menu and search buttons. [Launch film](https://github.com/VasuBansal7576/curbcut/blob/main/launch/curbcut-launch.mp4)

The extension bundles 128 fixes. On November 24, 2026, the installed-extension audit recorded 98 primary passes and seven unconfirmed results across that day’s 105 fixes. [Live audit](https://github.com/VasuBansal7576/curbcut/blob/main/proof/reliability/submission-extension-e2e.json) Anyone can open the popup and send a site owner the proposed change, the barrier it addresses and the supporting evidence. The long-term goal is for the site to work on its own. [Get Curb Cut for Chrome](https://github.com/VasuBansal7576/curbcut)
