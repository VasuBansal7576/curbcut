## Materials and sound

The physical object uses the landing page’s three photographed-style render layers. The native site text, icons, popup and recordings retain their captured shapes. Site recordings retain their source colours inside lilac frames. Terminal verdicts use Signal orange for failure and white for success. Isolated glyphs and extension cards use Damson and Mist. The contrast demonstration reconstructs the two measured text-to-background ratios using the original glyph mask. The screen-reader label gives the spoken English translation alongside the captured German name, Vorherige Folie.

The approved launch master includes the original voice, music and effects. The developer chapter uses Kokoro af_heart at the same 1.04 speed. Its 120 BPM music is synthesized locally from the same score, mixed under the new voice.
